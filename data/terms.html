What this is

MiniCDN is a learning and demonstration project that simulates how a content delivery network routes requests and caches files. Latencies, failures, and cache behaviour are generated by a model, not measured on a real network, and should not be used to judge the performance of any real service.

The demo store

The "MiniCDN Demo Store" page is sample content. Nothing on it is for sale, no orders or payments are taken, and the product names and prices in the sample data files are made up for testing. No refunds or cancellations apply because nothing can be purchased.
